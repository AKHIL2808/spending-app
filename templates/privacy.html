Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register, along with the expense records you log — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, like your browser type, needed to operate the service reliably.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate your account, display your expense history and summaries, and improve the reliability and usability of Spendly. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to anyone.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. Passwords are never stored in plain text. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which your data will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not share your expense data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on trusted third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting or email delivery services) solely to operate the application, and these providers are only given the minimum access necessary to perform their function.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
